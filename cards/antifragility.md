# Antifragility

Design systems that can improve from stress, volatility, and small shocks.

**Use when:** A workflow, habit, project, or career path is too fragile under uncertainty.

## Diagnostic questions

- What stress could make this stronger?
- Where is downside capped?
- Where is upside open-ended?
- What small experiment creates learning?

## Agent instruction

Add small controlled stressors, feedback loops, and capped downside bets.

## Failure mode

Confusing antifragility with recklessness or constant chaos.
