# Lindy Effect

For nonperishable ideas and tools, survival over time is evidence of durability.

**Use when:** Choosing books, practices, concepts, protocols, or long-lived tools.

## Diagnostic questions

- How long has this survived?
- Why did it persist?
- Is this domain Lindy-relevant?
- What recent alternative has unproven durability?

## Agent instruction

Prefer durable primitives when the cost of novelty is high.

## Failure mode

Rejecting new ideas blindly because old things feel safer.
