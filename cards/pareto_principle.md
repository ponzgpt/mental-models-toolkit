# Pareto Principle

A small share of causes often produces a large share of results.

**Use when:** You have too many tasks, clients, features, habits, or study topics.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which 20 percent creates most of the value?
- Which tasks create almost no value?
- What can be deleted, delegated, or delayed?
- Where is the real bottleneck?

## Agent instruction

Rank items by impact and protect the top few from low-value work.

## Failure mode

Using 80/20 as a slogan without measuring actual contribution.
