# Compounding / Snowball Effect

Small gains can become large when reinvested consistently over time.

**Use when:** Progress feels too slow, or you need a long-term accumulation strategy.

## Diagnostic questions

- What can compound here?
- What is the reinvestment loop?
- What breaks the loop?
- How will I measure trajectory rather than mood?

## Agent instruction

Define a repeatable input and a compounding asset it improves.

## Failure mode

Expecting compounding before enough repetitions have occurred.
