# Regret Minimization

Choose by asking which option your future self is least likely to regret.

**Use when:** A decision has emotional weight, uncertainty, or a long time horizon.

## Diagnostic questions

- At age 80, what would I wish I had tried?
- Which regret is recoverable?
- Which regret compounds if ignored?
- Am I avoiding discomfort or avoiding real risk?

## Agent instruction

Write the reversible and irreversible regrets, then pick the option with the better long-term regret profile.

## Failure mode

Romanticizing bold action when the real issue is unpriced downside.
