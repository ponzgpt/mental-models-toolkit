# 10-10-10 Rule

View a choice through three time windows: 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years.

**Use when:** Emotion is dominating a decision or a short-term discomfort hides long-term value.

## Diagnostic questions

- How will this feel in 10 minutes?
- What will matter in 10 months?
- What will still matter in 10 years?
- Which time window deserves priority here?

## Agent instruction

Score each option across the three windows and explain the tradeoff explicitly.

## Failure mode

Pretending every decision deserves a 10-year lens.
