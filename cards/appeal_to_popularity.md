# Appeal To Popularity

Popularity can indicate attention, not truth or usefulness.

**Use when:** A trend, tool, belief, or creator feels credible mainly because many people agree.

## Diagnostic questions

- What is the underlying evidence?
- Who benefits if I follow the crowd?
- Is popularity caused by quality or distribution?
- What would I think in private?

## Agent instruction

Separate social proof from the actual claim and test the claim directly.

## Failure mode

Ignoring useful crowd signals just to feel independent.
