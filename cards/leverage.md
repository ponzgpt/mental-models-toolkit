# Leverage

Use tools, code, media, capital, people, or processes to multiply output per unit of effort.

**Use when:** A valuable action is repeated manually or limited by personal time.

## Diagnostic questions

- What repeats?
- What can be automated, delegated, documented, or productized?
- What asset keeps working after I stop?
- What leverage has low downside?

## Agent instruction

Turn one repeated action into a reusable asset.

## Failure mode

Chasing leverage before the base action is valuable.
