# Information Arbitrage

Value appears when you know something useful before, deeper than, or across domains better than others.

**Use when:** Looking for career edge, content ideas, investing insight, or strategic positioning.

## Diagnostic questions

- What do I know that this audience does not?
- What domain can be translated into another?
- What signal is underpriced?
- How can I validate the edge?

## Agent instruction

Create one artifact that transfers useful knowledge across contexts.

## Failure mode

Mistaking obscure information for valuable information.
