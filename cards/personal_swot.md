# Personal SWOT

Map strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats before choosing a path.

**Use when:** You need a grounded view of fit, positioning, or personal strategy.

## Diagnostic questions

- What advantage do I repeatedly underuse?
- What weakness keeps recurring?
- What opportunity is visible now?
- What external threat can I prepare for?

## Agent instruction

Create a four-quadrant note and turn one item from each quadrant into an action.

## Failure mode

Writing flattering traits instead of evidence-backed patterns.
