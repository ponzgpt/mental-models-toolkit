# Commitment Bias

Prior commitments can pressure you to stay consistent after facts change.

**Use when:** You defend an old decision, public opinion, project, or identity despite weak evidence.

## Diagnostic questions

- What would I believe if I had not said this publicly?
- What new evidence matters?
- What identity is attached to this choice?
- What update would be honorable?

## Agent instruction

State the new evidence and choose whether to recommit, revise, or exit.

## Failure mode

Changing positions constantly to avoid accountability.
