# Second-Order Thinking

Evaluate the consequences of the consequences before choosing an action.

**Use when:** A decision has delayed effects, incentives, dependencies, or social consequences.

## Diagnostic questions

- What happens immediately if this works?
- What happens six weeks later?
- Who changes behavior because of this decision?
- What new problem does the solution create?

## Agent instruction

Write first-order, second-order, and third-order effects before committing.

## Failure mode

Stopping at the obvious benefit and missing the downstream cost.
