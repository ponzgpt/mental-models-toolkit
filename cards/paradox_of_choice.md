# Paradox Of Choice

Too many options can reduce action quality and increase dissatisfaction.

**Use when:** You are stuck comparing tools, careers, courses, prompts, apps, or plans.

## Diagnostic questions

- What decision criteria matter?
- What options can be removed?
- What is good enough?
- When does more research stop paying?

## Agent instruction

Set criteria, cap options, pick, and schedule a review point.

## Failure mode

Using choice limits to avoid exploring genuinely different options.
