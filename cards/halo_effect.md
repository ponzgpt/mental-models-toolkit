# Halo Effect

One positive trait can distort judgment of unrelated traits.

**Use when:** Evaluating people, tools, companies, creators, or your own work.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which trait is dominating my view?
- What evidence is independent of the halo?
- What would I think if the brand were removed?
- Where might I be overgeneralizing?

## Agent instruction

Separate the observed trait from the claim being evaluated.

## Failure mode

Replacing judgment with cynicism toward anything impressive.
