# Sunk Cost / Concorde Fallacy

Past investment should not justify future investment if the forward case is weak.

**Use when:** You keep funding, building, studying, or defending something mainly because you already started.

## Diagnostic questions

- Would I choose this again today?
- What future value remains?
- What am I afraid to admit?
- What would I recover by stopping?

## Agent instruction

Ignore past cost, evaluate future expected value, and define a stop rule.

## Failure mode

Quitting only because the past feels painful rather than because the future case is bad.
