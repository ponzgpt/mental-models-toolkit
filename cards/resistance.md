# Resistance

The strongest avoidance often appears near work that matters.

**Use when:** You repeatedly delay writing, publishing, studying, building, or asking.

## Diagnostic questions

- What task am I circling but not doing?
- What emotion appears before avoidance?
- What is the next physical action?
- Can I reduce the start to two minutes?

## Agent instruction

Name the avoided action and start a tiny version immediately.

## Failure mode

Turning resistance into identity instead of treating it as a signal.
