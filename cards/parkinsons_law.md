# Parkinsons Law

Work expands to fill the time available for its completion.

**Use when:** Tasks sprawl, deadlines are loose, or perfectionism delays shipping.

## Diagnostic questions

- What is the smallest useful version?
- What deadline would force clarity?
- What can be cut without breaking the outcome?
- Where am I polishing instead of deciding?

## Agent instruction

Set a hard timebox and define the deliverable before starting.

## Failure mode

Creating artificial urgency for work that actually needs deep thinking.
