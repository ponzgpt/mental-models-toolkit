# Hindsight Bias

After an outcome, events can look more predictable than they were.

**Use when:** Reviewing a win, failure, market move, relationship outcome, or project result.

## Diagnostic questions

- What did I actually know then?
- What probability did I assign?
- Which signals were ambiguous?
- What would improve the next forecast?

## Agent instruction

Keep decision logs with predictions before outcomes arrive.

## Failure mode

Using hindsight to punish uncertainty instead of improving judgment.
