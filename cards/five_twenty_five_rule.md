# 5/25 Rule

Identify the top five priorities and avoid the next twenty until the top five move.

**Use when:** You are scattered across too many goals or side projects.

## Diagnostic questions

- What are my top 25 goals?
- Which five matter most now?
- Which attractive goals must become avoid-at-all-costs?
- What will I say no to this week?

## Agent instruction

Write the top five and an avoid list; review both weekly.

## Failure mode

Treating the avoid list as temporary guilt instead of a real constraint.
